How do I make my CV ATS-friendly for jobs in Darwin?

Use a simple one-column layout, standard section headings, the job title and skills from the job description, and plain text instead of tables or graphics for key details. Send it in the format the employer asks for.

Is professional CV writing worth it?

It is worth it when your experience is strong but your CV does not show it clearly. A professional writer can improve structure, wording and role alignment. No CV service can guarantee interviews or jobs.

What is the difference between an ATS CV and a Europass CV?

An ATS CV is a clean, role-aligned CV that applicant tracking systems can read. A Europass CV is the standard European format, best used when a European employer or programme asks for it.
