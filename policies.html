Refund & cancellation

Once work on your order has started, payments are non-refundable.

Before we begin, we confirm the service, scope, timeline and total price with you. Work is considered started once payment is received and we begin reviewing your information or drafting your materials.

If you cancel after paying but before work has started, contact us right away at hello@skilleaze.com or on WhatsApp and we will review your request.

If we are unable to deliver a confirmed service, we will discuss a fair resolution with you.

Scope and pricing. Prices are shown on our website in the listed currency. The exact deliverables, timeline and total are confirmed with you before payment. Work outside the agreed scope may be charged separately, and we will tell you before doing it.

Your information. You agree to provide accurate details. We only use information you give us and never invent experience, qualifications, certifications or achievements. You are responsible for reviewing the final materials before using them.

Corrections. If something within the agreed scope is incorrect, tell us and we will correct it. Any revision terms for your order are confirmed before payment.

No guaranteed outcomes. We improve how you present yourself. We cannot guarantee jobs, interviews, recruiter contact or results from any applicant tracking system, as those decisions are made by employers.

Account access. If you share login access for LinkedIn or a job portal, we use it only to complete your order and log out when done. You can change your password at any time.

Third-party platforms. Skilleaze is independent and not affiliated with LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, Bayt, Naukrigulf, GulfTalent, foundit, Shine or any other platform.

Privacy policy

What we collect. Your name, contact details, CV, work history, education, skills, target roles and any documents or profile links you share with us.

How we use it. Only to deliver and support your order, communicate with you and improve our services.

Who can see it. Only authorized Skilleaze team members working on your order. We do not sell your information.

Reviews and examples. We never publish your messages, documents or details without your permission, and we hide names and personal information when we do.

Payments. Never share card details in chat. Payment instructions are sent only through our official channels.

Your choices. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing hello@skilleaze.com.
