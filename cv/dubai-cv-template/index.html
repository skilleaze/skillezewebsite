Dubai CV Template: what you should know

Details UAE employers look for. Current location, visa status, notice period and nationality are commonly included, along with a professional photo if you choose.

Keep it focused. Two pages is normal for experienced candidates. Lead with your most relevant Gulf or international experience.

ATS-friendly by design. Many large employers in the UAE use applicant tracking systems, so we keep the structure simple and the keywords relevant.

A strong dubai CV template balances what Gulf recruiters want to see quickly (role, location, visa and availability) with the substance that gets you shortlisted: clear achievements, relevant tools and skills, and a profile written for the job.
