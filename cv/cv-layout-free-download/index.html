CV Layout Free Download: what you should know

Choose a layout that ATS can read. Single-column structure, standard headings (Profile, Experience, Education, Skills) and real text instead of images or text boxes.

Keep it to one or two pages. Freshers usually need one page; experienced professionals can use two. Every line should support the job you are applying for.

Word or PDF? Send PDF unless the employer asks for Word. We deliver both, so you are covered either way.

A good CV layout free download is a starting point, not the finish line. The layouts that work best are calm and consistent: clear section headings, dates aligned on one side, enough white space, and no tables, icons or graphics that can confuse applicant tracking systems. What makes a CV stand out is what goes inside it: a short profile written for the role, achievements with numbers where you have them, and skills that match the job description.
