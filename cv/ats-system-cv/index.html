ATS System CV: what you should know

How ATS reads a CV. It extracts your text into fields such as name, job titles, dates and skills, then recruiters search those fields by keyword.

What makes a CV ATS-friendly. Real text, a single-column layout, standard headings and keywords that match the job, with no tables, icons or text boxes.

What ATS doesn't do. It doesn't decide alone. People still read and choose; ATS only makes sure your CV can be found and read.

Understanding ATS system CV helps you avoid the most common mistakes: designs exported as images, important details hidden in headers, creative section names and missing role keywords.
