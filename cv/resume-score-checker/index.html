Resume Score Checker: what you should know

What a checker can tell you. Whether your text can be read, whether common sections exist, and which keywords from a job description appear in your CV.

What it can't. Whether your achievements are convincing, whether your summary fits the role, or how a recruiter will feel after reading the first few lines.

What we fix. Layout problems, missing keywords, weak bullet points and unclear job titles, using only information that is true about you.

People search for an resume score checker because they are applying and not hearing back. The cause is rarely a single number. It is usually a mix of formatting that some systems struggle to read, keywords that do not match the role, and experience written as duties instead of results. A score can point to the problem; it cannot rewrite the CV for you. Share your current CV with us on WhatsApp, tell us the role you want, and our expert will rebuild it in a clean ATS-friendly format.
