SERVICE DETAILS

About our Europass CV service

Europass is a standard European CV format used for many applications across the EU, especially for public-sector roles, academic programmes, internships and some private employers. It presents personal details, work experience, education, language skills and digital skills in a consistent structure.

A Skilleaze expert organises your information into a complete, well-written Europass CV, including correct language levels on the CEFR scale. We will also tell you honestly when a standard ATS CV suits your target employer better, and we always follow the instructions in the job advertisement.

What’s included

  • Collection and structuring of your career information
  • Work experience and education written clearly in Europass layout
  • Language skills mapped to CEFR levels (A1–C2)
  • Digital and job-related skills presented professionally
  • Consistency check and delivery in the agreed format

Who it’s for

Candidates applying for jobs, studies or internships in Europe, and anyone whose application specifically asks for a Europass CV.
