About Skilleaze · Est. 2025

We don’t just create documents. We build profiles that open doors.

Skilleaze is a career advisory platform focused on how professionals present themselves in today’s competitive job market.

We craft ATS-optimized CVs and offer complete career support to make you more visible and better positioned. Every solution pairs strategic insight with a deep understanding of recruitment systems and hiring behavior.

Our story

Built from real experience in Dubai.

Most job seekers don’t lack capability. They lack the structure, positioning and presentation to stand out.

Working in Dubai’s highly competitive job market, we kept seeing the same gap between talent and opportunity. Capable professionals struggled to get interview calls, while a more strategic approach to applications was consistently opening doors for us.

The difference wasn’t qualifications alone. It was how profiles were presented, how applications were positioned, and how well ATS and recruiter search behavior were understood.

It started with helping a small circle of friends: refining their CVs, sharpening their professional presence and guiding their applications. The improvement was clear and repeatable. That became the foundation of Skilleaze.

Skilleaze was officially established in 2025, with a focus on high-quality, practical career support that stays affordable. Professional help shouldn’t be limited by financial barriers.
