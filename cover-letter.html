About our cover letter writing

A good cover letter explains why you fit a specific role in a way a CV cannot. It connects your experience to what the employer needs, shows genuine interest in the organisation and gives a recruiter a reason to read your CV properly.

Our experts write a focused, one-page letter based on your CV and, ideally, the job description. We avoid generic templates and repeated CV lines. Instead, we pick two or three pieces of evidence that matter most for the position and present them in a confident, professional tone.

What’s included

  • A tailored opening that names the role
  • Two or three evidence-based reasons you are a strong fit
  • Alignment with the employer and job description where available
  • A clear, professional closing and call to action
  • Proofreading and delivery in Word and PDF

Who it’s for

Job seekers applying to roles where a cover letter is requested or expected, and candidates who want a stronger first impression for competitive vacancies.
