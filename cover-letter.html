Professional Cover Letter Writing

A one-page letter that connects your experience to the job, complements your CV instead of repeating it, and sounds like a real person wrote it.

What's included

  • Tailored to the job description you share
  • Strong opening that names the role
  • Two or three proof points from your experience
  • Professional close with a clear next step
  • Proofread and formatted
  • PDF and editable Word

Staring at a blank page again?

  • “I am writing to apply for…”: An opening that earns the next line.
  • Your CV, repeated in paragraphs: New context your CV can’t show.
  • One letter for every company: Written for this role and employer.

The letter is where you explain the fit.

When employers ask for a cover letter, it’s your chance to connect the dots between your experience and their needs. A generic one wastes that chance.

  • Specific. References the role and what it actually needs.
  • Evidence-based. Claims backed by your real experience.
  • Concise. One page, easy to read.
  • On-brand with your CV. Same story, same tone, no contradictions.

What we need from you

Your CV, the job title and description, the company name if known, and why you want the role.

Who it's for

Specific applications, Career changers, Senior roles, Fresh graduates.

Frequently asked questions

We can write a general professional letter, but a tailored one works best. Ask us about tailoring for multiple roles.

Do you need the job description?

It helps a lot. Without it we write a strong general letter for your target role.

Is it included with the CV?

It’s priced separately so you only pay for what you need. We send an itemized total for combined orders.
