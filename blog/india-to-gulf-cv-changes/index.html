Moving From India to the Gulf: CV Changes to Make

What to adjust when applying for jobs in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and the wider GCC from India.

Add Gulf-specific details

Nationality, visa status, notice period and availability to relocate are commonly checked first.

Use international job titles

Replace internal or regional titles with standard ones Gulf recruiters search for.

Highlight relevant sectors

Gulf hiring is strong in construction, hospitality, finance, healthcare, energy and logistics. Emphasise experience that fits.

Consider a photo

Professional photos are common on Gulf CVs, unlike many Western markets.

Update your portals

Create or refresh profiles on Bayt, Naukrigulf, GulfTalent and LinkedIn with your target location.

Frequently asked questions

Should I convert my salary to the local currency?

You usually do not need to list salary on a Gulf CV. If a form asks for it, give your current salary in the original currency and be ready to discuss expectations in the local currency once you understand the package.

Do I need my degree attested for Gulf jobs?

Many Gulf countries require degree attestation for work visas, particularly for professional roles. Mention on your CV if your documents are already attested, as it can signal that you are ready to move quickly.

Should I list my passport details on my CV?

Mentioning nationality and that you hold a valid passport is helpful. Do not add your passport number or other sensitive identity details to a CV you send widely; share those only when an employer formally requests them.

General guidance only. Employer requirements vary; always follow the job advert.
