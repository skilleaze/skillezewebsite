Moving From India to the Gulf: CV Changes to Make

What to adjust when applying for jobs in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and the wider GCC from India.

Add Gulf-specific details

Nationality, visa status, notice period and availability to relocate are commonly checked first.

Use international job titles

Replace internal or regional titles with standard ones Gulf recruiters search for.

Highlight relevant sectors

Gulf hiring is strong in construction, hospitality, finance, healthcare, energy and logistics. Emphasise experience that fits.

Consider a photo

Professional photos are common on Gulf CVs, unlike many Western markets.

Update your portals

Create or refresh profiles on Bayt, Naukrigulf, GulfTalent and LinkedIn with your target location.
