Cover Letters: When They Matter and How to Write One

A short guide to writing a cover letter that adds value instead of repeating your CV.

When it matters

Cover letters matter most for competitive roles, career changes, international applications and when the advert asks for one.

Open with the role and a hook

Name the job and give one strong reason you fit in the first two lines.

Show evidence

Show you know the employer

Mention something specific about the company or team and why it appeals to you.
