How Applicant Tracking Systems Actually Read Your CV

What an ATS does with your file, why good candidates get filtered out, and simple fixes that help.

What an ATS is

An applicant tracking system is software employers use to collect, store and filter applications. It turns your CV into structured data: name, contact details, job titles, dates, skills and education.

Why formatting matters

If the system cannot read a section, that information may be missing when a recruiter searches. Graphics, headers and footers, columns and unusual fonts are common causes.

How keyword search works

Recruiters often search the database for job titles and skills. If the exact terms they use are not in your CV, you may not appear in results, even if you are qualified. Use the wording from job descriptions where it truthfully describes your experience.

What no one can promise

Every employer configures its system differently. No CV can be guaranteed to pass every ATS. The goal is to make your information as easy as possible to read correctly.

Quick checklist

Single column, standard headings, consistent dates, plain bullet points, relevant keywords, and a file name that includes your name and target role.

Frequently asked questions

Can an ATS read PDF files?

Most modern systems read text-based PDFs well. Problems start when the PDF is a scanned image, uses unusual fonts, or places text inside tables, text boxes or graphics. Export your PDF directly from Word or Google Docs and check that you can select and copy the text.

Do columns break an ATS?

Some systems read two-column layouts in the wrong order, mixing dates and job titles. A single-column layout with standard headings such as Experience, Education and Skills is the lowest-risk choice when you do not know which system an employer uses.

How many keywords should I add?

There is no magic number. Use the terms that genuinely describe your work and match the job description, placed naturally in your summary, skills and experience. Repeating words or hiding text in white font can look like manipulation to the recruiter who reads it next.
