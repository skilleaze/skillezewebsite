Career Change CV: How to Show Transferable Skills

How to present your experience when moving into a new industry or role.

Lead with your target role

Your profile should state where you are going, not just where you have been.

Group transferable skills

Communication, project management, analysis and leadership carry across industries. Show where you used them.

Reframe past roles

Frequently asked questions

Which CV format is best for a career change?

A combination format often works best: a strong summary naming your target role, a section of relevant skills with examples, then your work history. Avoid purely skills-based CVs that hide dates, because recruiters often distrust them.

Should I take a course before changing careers?

A relevant certificate or project can make a big difference, because it shows commitment and gives you concrete evidence. Choose recognised courses and, more importantly, apply what you learn in a project you can talk about.

How do I explain my career change in interviews?

Keep it positive and forward-looking: what draws you to the new field, what you have done to prepare and how your past experience helps. Practise a short, confident answer of about a minute.
