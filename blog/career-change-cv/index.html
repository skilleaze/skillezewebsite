Career Change CV: How to Show Transferable Skills

How to present your experience when moving into a new industry or role.

Lead with your target role

Your profile should state where you are going, not just where you have been.

Group transferable skills

Communication, project management, analysis and leadership carry across industries. Show where you used them.

Add relevant learning

Reframe past roles

Use a cover letter
