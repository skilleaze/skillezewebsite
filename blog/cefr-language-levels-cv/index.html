CEFR Language Levels Explained for Your CV

How to describe your language skills correctly using A1 to C2.

The six levels

A1 and A2 are basic, B1 and B2 are independent, C1 and C2 are proficient. Employers in Europe use these levels to compare candidates.

How to choose your level

Be honest. If you can handle work meetings and emails comfortably, you are likely B2 or above. Certificates help support your claim.

Where to show it

Europass has a dedicated section. On a standard CV, add a short Languages line with the level for each language.

Mother tongue

List your native language as such rather than giving it a level.

Why accuracy matters

Interviews often test language ability. Overstating your level can cost you the offer.
