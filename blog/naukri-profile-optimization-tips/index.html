Frequently asked questions

How often should I update my Naukri profile?

Recruiters often sort by recently active or updated profiles. Making a genuine update every week or two, such as refining a skill or adding a project, keeps your profile fresh. Avoid meaningless edits; keep changes accurate and useful.

How many key skills should I add on Naukri?

Add the skills you actually use and that appear in jobs you want, usually around ten to fifteen. Order them by importance and use the same names recruiters use in job adverts, for example specific tools rather than vague terms.

Does salary information affect visibility?

Recruiters filter by current and expected salary, so leaving it blank or unrealistic can reduce relevant matches. Enter honest figures, and you can control visibility settings if you prefer not to show them publicly.
