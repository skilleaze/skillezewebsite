About personal portfolio websites

A CV lists what you have done; a portfolio shows it. A personal website gives recruiters one simple link to see your projects, case studies, designs, writing or code alongside your experience and skills.

We design and build a clean, mobile-friendly portfolio around your real work. You choose a design direction, share your content and projects, and we structure them into clear sections with a contact option, so you have a professional home online to share in applications, on LinkedIn and in your email signature.

What’s included

  • Introduction, about and skills sections
  • Experience and project or case-study pages
  • Mobile-responsive design in the Skilleaze style you choose
  • Contact section or form
  • Domain and hosting options explained before we start

Who it’s for

Designers, developers, marketers, writers, freshers with strong projects and any professional whose work is easier to show than describe.
