About Shine profile optimization

Shine is a widely used Indian job portal for freshers and experienced professionals. Recruiters search it by keyword, location, notice period and experience, so the wording and completeness of your profile directly affect whether you appear for the right roles.

A Skilleaze expert rewrites your Shine headline, profile summary, experience and key skills for the role you are targeting, using the terms recruiters actually search for. We keep your Shine profile consistent with your CV and LinkedIn, and recommend how to complete every field that matters on the platform.

What’s included

  • Review of your current Shine profile
  • Headline and profile summary rewritten for your target role
  • Experience and key skills refined with relevant keywords
  • Consistency with your CV and other profiles
  • Advice on fields, preferences and attachments
