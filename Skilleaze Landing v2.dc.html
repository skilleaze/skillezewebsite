Good questions.

ATS CV or Europass: which one?+

Depends on your target country, role and the employer’s instructions. Europass isn’t right for every European employer. Tell us where you’re applying and we’ll recommend one.

Will my CV pass every ATS?+

No one can honestly guarantee that. Employers configure systems differently. We use a clean structure and relevant terminology so your CV is easier to parse and read.

What do I need to send?+

Your current CV if you have one, work history with dates, education, certifications, skills, target role, target country and any genuine achievements. For a cover letter, the job description helps.

How long does it take? Revisions?+

We confirm the timeline and revision terms for your order before you pay. No guessing.

Who runs the interview sessions?+

Industry leaders, one-to-one, in a one-hour session focused on your target role.
