# Skilleaze: SEO tasks only you can do

## Week 1: foundations
1. Publish: unzip all zips, copy assets/kw.css, sk-player.js, robots.txt, llms.txt, sitemap-index.xml and 9f3c2a7e5b1d4c8fa6e0b2d7c4a19e53.txt to the root, push.
2. Google Search Console: verify skilleaze.com (Domain property via DNS). Submit sitemap-index.xml. Use URL Inspection to request indexing for the home page, /locations/ and your top 20 city pages.
3. Bing Webmaster Tools: import from Search Console, submit sitemap-index.xml. Bing feeds ChatGPT search and Copilot.
4. IndexNow (Bing, Yandex, Seznam, Naver): after each push open
   https://www.bing.com/indexnow?url=https://www.skilleaze.com/&key=9f3c2a7e5b1d4c8fa6e0b2d7c4a19e53
5. Google Business Profile: create "Skilleaze" at your Puttur office (category: Career counselor / Resume service). Add photos of the office, hours, WhatsApp, website. This is the biggest local ranking factor.
6. Ask your happy clients for Google reviews (send them the GBP review link on WhatsApp after delivery). Real reviews are the only safe way to show a star rating.

## Ratings: important
Do not add a 4.9 rating to the website schema until it reflects real, verifiable reviews. Google treats self-written ratings on your own business pages as spam and can remove all rich results or penalise the site. Once your Google Business Profile has a real average (e.g. 4.9 from 40 reviews), tell me and I will show that number on the pages as visible text linking to the reviews.

## Month 1: authority (off-page)
7. Listings with the same name, address, phone and website everywhere (NAP consistency): Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, JustDial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Yelp, Trustpilot, Clutch (for portfolio sites), Crunchbase, LinkedIn company page, Facebook, Instagram.
8. Trustpilot or Google reviews widget: collect reviews there too; AI assistants cite third-party review sites.
9. Guest articles and features: career blogs, college placement cells, Gulf expat communities, LinkedIn articles. Each should link to one city or keyword page.
10. Partnerships: colleges in Karnataka/Kerala, GCC recruitment agencies, coaching institutes. Ask for a "career partner" link.
11. Quora, Reddit (r/resumes, r/dubai, r/india), LinkedIn posts: answer CV and ATS questions honestly and link to the matching keyword page when it genuinely helps.
12. YouTube: short videos (CV tips, before/after formatting with consent). Link to the site in descriptions. Google and AI tools pull from YouTube.

## Ongoing: GEO (AI search)
13. Keep llms.txt accurate when prices or services change.
14. Publish 2 helpful articles a month (e.g. "ATS CV format for UAE jobs 2026", "Europass CV for Germany"). AI tools cite clear, factual guides.
15. Get mentioned (with a link) on sites AI tools trust: news, Wikipedia-style directories, industry lists, podcasts.
16. Test monthly: ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot "best ATS CV writing service in Dubai" and similar; note whether Skilleaze appears.

## Monthly: measure
17. Search Console: pages indexed, top queries, cities getting impressions. Improve pages that get impressions but no clicks (title/description).
18. Remove or noindex city pages that get zero impressions after 6 months to keep the site quality high.
19. Track WhatsApp chats by source (ask "how did you find us?").
