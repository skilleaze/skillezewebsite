About our ATS-optimized CV writing

Most employers now screen applications with an applicant tracking system before a recruiter reads them. A CV with tables, text boxes, icons or unusual headings can lose information when the system parses it, so a qualified candidate may never be seen.

A Skilleaze expert rebuilds your CV around your real experience and the role you are targeting. We use a clean single-column structure, standard section headings, role-relevant keywords and achievement-focused bullet points, then check names, dates and contact details before delivery. No ATS can be guaranteed, but a well-structured CV is easier for both software and people to read.

What’s included

  • Review of your current CV, if you have one
  • Professional summary written for your target role
  • Experience rewritten as clear, measurable achievements
  • Skills and keywords aligned to the job market you are applying in
  • Final proofreading and delivery in Word and PDF

Who it’s for

Students and fresh graduates, early-career and mid-level professionals, career changers and anyone applying in India, the UAE, the wider GCC or internationally.
